description: 'Returning to Indonesia is not particularly unusual for me. It is, after all, home. This time, however, I returned in a different capacity: as an educator accompanying a group of students from DSI in China on a two-week conservation expedition with Operation Wallacea.'
category: Reflection
tags: []
---

As an Indonesian working abroad, I have often thought of returning home as something familiar. This expedition reminded me that familiarity does not necessarily mean understanding.

There is still so much of my own country to discover, and perhaps one of the privileges of being an educator is having the opportunity to discover it alongside those we teach.

For two weeks, the forest and the ocean became our classrooms. And I was a learner just as much as my students were.
